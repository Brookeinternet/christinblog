Our Mission

This hub is a place to celebrate the magic in the mundane. We focus on finding joy in simple moments—from baking colorful cakes and finding cozy home decor, to navigating the funny, chaotic world of having a little sister. Our goal is to create content that is authentic, heartwarming, and always fun.

Meet the Stars

Christina (The Big Sis)

The creator, expert chef, and chief builder behind the scenes. Christina plans the DIY projects, masters the dance trends, and attempts to maintain focus (mostly failing) when the music starts. She's focused on lifestyle, fitness, and design—and documenting every hilarious moment of toddlerhood.

Kinko (The Little Sis)

Kinko is the kind of child who doesn’t just wear a bear suit—she becomes the bear. Fuzzy, wide-eyed, and full of wonder, she’s the youngest—a little ember of curiosity wrapped in orange fleece. Her hood has ears, but her real antennae are emotional: tuned to every rustle of leaves, every blur beyond the glass. She doesn’t speak in full sentences yet, but her expressions are essays. Her surprise is sacred. Her awe is contagious.
